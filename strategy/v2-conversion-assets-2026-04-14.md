## Master Framing

SpeakLocal should be sold as a curated Vietnam travel companion, not a language-learning app, generic phrase list, Google Translate replacement, or arbitrary AI translation box.

Recommended master promise:

> Discover what to eat, where to go, and what to say in Vietnam, with curated phrases and playable Vietnamese audio.

Recommended supporting frame:

> Food, places, culture, useful phrases, and audio before and during the trip.

Recommended funnel sentence:

> Try SpeakLocal Vietnam free for 7 days. Keep the full curated Vietnam companion for `$4.99/month`.

Recommended reason to choose SpeakLocal:

> AI and translation apps can translate what you type. SpeakLocal gives travelers a curated Vietnam trip map first: food and menu items, places and things to do, useful phrases, local context, and audio they can actually hear before speaking.

## Audience

Primary:

- English-speaking travelers excited to go to Vietnam;
- first-time or early-stage Vietnam planners who want the country to feel more vivid and less intimidating;
- food, coffee, market, city, landmark, and culture-curious travelers;
- people who want practical phrases with audio without taking a formal course.

Not primary:

- classroom-style Vietnamese learners seeking fluency;
- users who only want arbitrary live translation;
- people looking for a legal, medical, or emergency authority;
- fear-led travelers being sold a panic product.

## Emotional Direction

Lead with:

- discovery;
- food and coffee;
- cities and places;
- culture and curiosity;
- useful local phrases;
- hearing the Vietnamese before trying it;
- feeling prepared before landing.

Avoid leading with:

- anxiety;
- safety-first positioning;
- panic;
- fear-heavy safety framing;
- being trapped or stuck;
- fluency or mastery.

Serious support surfaces can exist, but they should be calm, practical, and secondary to the positive travel experience.

## Offer Truth

Current offer:

- 7-day free trial;
- then `$4.99/month`;
- product ID: `app.speaklocal.vietnam.subscription.monthly`.

The trial should be earned by showing value first. Good first-run and paywall flows should let the traveler hear or save a useful phrase, see prepared Vietnam content, and understand that the app is curated before the subscription ask.

Do not write copy that implies:

- legacy fixed-price unlock language;
- old no-recurring-payment claims;
- a permanent free tier;
- a generic full-library unlock unrelated to the current monthly subscription direction.

## Paid Value

The subscription is worth paying for because it keeps the richer curated Vietnam experience available through the trip:

- 900 visible intent families / travel situations;
- 750 premium visible intent families;
- 919 approved phrase rows currently marked audio-ready;
- food, coffee, menu, and drink depth;
- city, place, landmark, and things-to-do context;
- culture-aware phrase pages;
- playable bundled audio;
- search, Browse, saved, and Practice loops;
- calm practical support for real travel moments.

Do not sell paid value as fear insurance. Sell it as the complete curated Vietnam companion in the traveler's pocket.

## Guardrails

- Lead with the destination, not the app mechanics.
- Keep Google Translate and AI comparisons respectful and specific: they translate what you ask; SpeakLocal curates what a Vietnam traveler should see, hear, and save.
- Do not imply arbitrary AI translation or live AI chat.
- Do not overpromise fluency.
- Do not overclaim legal, medical, safety, or emergency reliability.
- Keep serious trip-support copy useful and calm.
- Keep the visual story sensory: food, places, audio, phrases, saved trip set, and the native iPhone experience.

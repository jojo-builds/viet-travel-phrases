# Gate 2 Pass 1: Traveler Utility Review

Approval: BLOCK

## Summary

Urgent/help and service/document hubs feel practical, but the greetings/social set still carries too much template residue to clear the utility bar consistently.

## Findings

- Urgent/help hubs are compact and action-first.
- Service/document hubs feel believable and useful.
- Greetings/social hubs reuse the same opener-first bullets and `say-next` framing even when the traveler moment is `thank-you`, `no-thanks`, or recommendation-led.

## Suggested adjustments

- Rewrite the greetings/social core bullets and `say-next` summaries so each hub reflects its own traveler moment.
- Keep the current urgent/help and service/document tone.

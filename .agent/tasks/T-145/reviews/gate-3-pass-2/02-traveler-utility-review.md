# Gate 3 Pass 2: Traveler Utility Review

Approval: APPROVE

## Summary

The 24-hub packet reads like practical traveler answer-page data overall. It stays compact, pushes the traveler toward the next concrete move, and covers greetings/social, urgent-help/medical, repair/clarification, and service/navigation with distinct enough utility patterns to feel app-usable rather than textbook-like.

## Findings

- Urgent-help/medical is the strongest class: the hubs stay action-first, make escalation paths clear, and consistently tell the traveler when to move to ambulance, hospital, police, map, writing, or English help.
- Repair/clarification and service/navigation also feel genuinely useful because they focus on real trip friction points like numbers, map pins, exact drop-off points, printing, email handoff, and written backup instead of generic language-learning advice.
- Some greetings/social and a few pharmacy/print/email follow-up bullets still lean on broad family-summary wording such as "This helps you..." or "Use this when...", which softens the realism slightly, but not enough to create a real usefulness problem or justify a block.

## Suggested adjustments

- Optional: tighten the remaining family-summary-derived bullets into more situational traveler guidance, especially the pharmacy next-step copy and the print/email likely-reply lines.
- Optional: soften the pharmacy hub a little so it reads equally well for ordinary medication-finding moments, not just semi-emergency framing.

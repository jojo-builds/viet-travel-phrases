# Gate 1 Pass 2: Traveler Utility Review

Approval: APPROVE

## Summary

The tighter module and relation-depth rules improve the 24-hub plan without hurting traveler usefulness, so the plan is ready for authoring.

## Findings

- The four class mixes still feel practical and high-value.
- Urgent-help-medical now has stronger stress-case flow depth.
- The main utility risk is forcing artificial polite or clearer variants where they do not exist naturally.
- `service-print` and `service-email-file` remain acceptable if they stay anchored to real ticket, form, or reservation moments.

## Suggested adjustments

- Treat clearer and polite alternates as optional in high-stress hubs.
- Keep urgent/help modules action-first and very short.

# Gate 1 Pass 1: Traveler Utility Review

Approval: APPROVE

## Summary

The planned 24-hub set is practical and high-value enough to support traveler-useful answer pages across distinct phrase classes.

## Findings

- The four class-specific module mixes are meaningfully different and avoid a one-shape-fits-all answer page.
- Emergency, medical, repair, transport, and directions coverage is strong and traveler-realistic.
- `social-how-are-you`, `service-print`, and `service-email-file` are the weakest priority calls if a swap becomes helpful.

## Suggested adjustments

- Keep urgent/help modules very short under stress.
- Ensure every urgent/help hub has one obvious next tap and one repair fallback.

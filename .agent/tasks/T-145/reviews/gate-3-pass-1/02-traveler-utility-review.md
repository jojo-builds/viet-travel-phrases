# Gate 3 Pass 1: Traveler Utility Review

Approval: APPROVE

## Summary

The 24-hub packet reads like practical traveler answer-page data overall. The greetings/social hubs stay brief and situational, the urgent-help/medical hubs stay action-first, the repair/clarification hubs tell the traveler when to switch methods, and the service/navigation hubs consistently focus on what to show, what detail to confirm, and what to do next.

## Findings

- The strongest utility is in the urgent-help/medical and repair/clarification hubs, which consistently steer the traveler toward the next concrete move instead of drifting into textbook explanation.
- The service/navigation hubs feel worth including in the packet because they cover real traveler friction points like showing a map pin, confirming drop-off details, printing documents, and emailing files at informal counters.
- Some `likely-reply` or follow-up bullets in greetings/social and a few medical/service hubs still lean on generic family-summary wording instead of the more situational relation note, but the remaining abstraction is not severe enough to block usefulness.

## Suggested adjustments

- Optional: tighten the remaining `likely-reply` and follow-up bullets so they keep the scenario-specific note visible, especially where the current wording collapses into broad summaries like number repair or polite acknowledgment.
- Optional: soften the pharmacy hub’s emergency-style framing slightly so it reads more like practical medical navigation than a fully urgent escalation path.

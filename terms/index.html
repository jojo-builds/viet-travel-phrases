Terms

Terms of use

These terms cover the SpeakLocal website, app, Starter access, and optional Apple subscription.

Travel information

SpeakLocal content is general travel information. Local conditions, prices, business policies, language usage, and service availability can change. Verify critical decisions with an appropriate local or official source, especially for health, safety, legal, financial, or emergency matters.

Starter access

The app can be opened and used in Starter without a subscription or a SpeakLocal account. Starter includes the free content and features identified in the app. Full Access offers are optional and can be dismissed.

Full Access subscription and one-week trial

Full Access is an optional monthly auto-renewable subscription that opens the additional app content and features identified in the offer. Eligible new U.S. subscribers receive a one-week free trial. The prepared U.S. offer is $4.99 per month after the trial. Apple determines introductory-offer eligibility and shows the authoritative localized price, currency, and any applicable tax before you confirm.

If you start the one-week trial, Full Access renews at the displayed monthly price when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. After that, the subscription renews monthly at the displayed price until cancelled. Payment is charged to the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Manage or cancel

You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings. Apple advises cancelling a free or discounted trial at least 24 hours before it ends. For later monthly periods, use the renewal date shown in Apple Account settings to cancel before the next charge. After cancellation, Full Access normally remains available through the end of the paid period. Deleting SpeakLocal does not cancel a subscription.

Restore purchases

Use Restore or Restore Purchases on a Full Access screen in the app to ask Apple to restore an eligible purchase made with the same Apple Account. Restore does not start a new purchase.

Billing and refunds

Apple processes App Store payments, renewals, cancellations, and refunds. SpeakLocal cannot issue an App Store refund directly. To request a refund or review a charge, use Apple’s Report a Problem service or Apple billing support.

Privacy and support

Read the Privacy Policy for information about app and website data. For subscription, access, or product help, use the SpeakLocal support route.

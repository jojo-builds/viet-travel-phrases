# Two-Week Launch Channel Plan

Date: 2026-07-05
Status: execution-ready draft, dependent on final screenshots and App Store/TestFlight link
Primary audience: English-speaking Vietnam travelers planning before arrival

## Launch Goal

Get the first qualified travelers to understand SpeakLocal Vietnam as a curated travel companion: food, places, useful phrases, saved/practice loops, and playable Vietnamese audio.

The first two weeks should learn which wedge converts best:

- Food/menu confidence.
- Before-you-land phrase readiness.
- Search the trip moment.
- City/place discovery.
- Practice without school pressure.

## First Paid Test Concepts

Run only after organic/screenshot assets prove the core screens.

### Concept A: Food First

- Audience segment: food-curious first-time Vietnam travelers.
- User problem or moment: They want to enjoy food but menu names feel opaque.
- Hook: `Know the dish before you point at the menu.`
- Channel: TikTok/Reels paid test, later Apple Search Ads.
- Offer or CTA: Download before Vietnam.
- Required app evidence: food/menu screenshot and audio tap recording.
- Claim risk: avoid implying full menu translation or all dishes covered.
- Success metric: thumb-stop, saves, App Store taps, install conversion.
- Next action: capture supported dish route.

### Concept B: Before-You-Land

- Audience segment: planners within 60 days of travel.
- User problem or moment: They want useful phrases without studying.
- Hook: `Land in Vietnam with the phrases you need first.`
- Channel: Reels, Shorts, Apple Search Ads.
- Offer or CTA: Download before the trip.
- Required app evidence: Home, Search, Save, Practice.
- Claim risk: avoid fluency and emergency claims.
- Success metric: install conversion and first save.
- Next action: record Home to Search to Save flow.

## Measurement

- App Store product page views.
- Product page conversion rate.
- Keyword impressions and tap-through.
- Organic saves, shares, comments, profile taps, and App Store clicks.
- First in-app search, first detail open, first save, first Practice open.
- Trial starts and purchase conversion only after `NEEDS_STOREKIT_PROOF` clears.
- Top repeated objection from comments/reviews.

Date: 2026-07-05

Owner lane: `marketing/`
Product truth: native iOS `main`, current operational docs, and `native-ios/Config/apps/vietnam.json`

## Purpose

This project turns the current native SpeakLocal Vietnam app truth into executable App Store and launch materials without inventing unreleased features.

The customer-facing promise is:

> SpeakLocal Vietnam helps travelers discover what to eat, where to go, and what to say, with curated Vietnam trip phrases, useful context, saved/practice loops, and playable Vietnamese audio.

Do not position the app as a live translator, arbitrary AI translation box, general Vietnamese course, fluency product, emergency authority, or medical/legal tool.

## Assets In This Pack

- `app-store-metadata.md` - App Store name, subtitle, keywords, promotional text, long description, release notes, and submission fields.
- `screenshot-storyboard.md` - App Store screenshot sequence tied to current native routes and proof needs.
- `app-preview-30s-shot-list.md` - 30-second App Preview script and capture plan.
- `two-week-launch-channel-plan.md` - first 14 days of launch channels, content, metrics, and next actions.
- `claim-risk-checklist.md` - publishable, careful, blocked, and forbidden claims tied to app evidence.
- `source-and-evidence-register.md` - repo evidence and Apple spec sources used.

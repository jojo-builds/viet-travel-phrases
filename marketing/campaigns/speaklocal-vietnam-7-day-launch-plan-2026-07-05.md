# SpeakLocal Vietnam 7-Day Launch Plan

Date: 2026-07-05
Status: execution-ready marketing plan, pending final screenshots and StoreKit proof
Primary audience: English-speaking Vietnam travelers planning before arrival

## Positioning

SpeakLocal Vietnam helps excited travelers know what to eat, where to go, and what to say, with native iOS browsing, search, saved trip phrases, Practice, and playable Vietnamese audio.

This is not a live translator, a school course, or a fluency promise. It is a prepared Vietnam companion for the food, places, sounds, and phrases that make the trip feel more vivid before landing.

## Target Traveler

- Segment: first-time or early-stage Vietnam travelers, mostly English-speaking, planning from home or already on the way.
- Travel moment: they have a trip in mind and want Vietnam to feel less abstract: food names, coffee, places, airport/hotel/taxi basics, and words they can hear.
- Job: help them feel oriented enough to save a few phrases, open a city or food page, and believe the full app belongs on the trip.

## Objections And Answers

| Objection | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| I already have Google Translate. | SpeakLocal is not trying to replace live translation. It helps you know what to search for, what to eat, where to go, and which trip phrases are worth hearing and saving before you need them. |
| I do not want to study Vietnamese. | You do not have to. Open the food, place, or situation, hear the phrase, and save what fits your trip. |
| Will this make me fluent? | No. It is for useful travel readiness, not fluency. |
| Can I use it in emergencies? | It includes calm help, health, pharmacy, and support phrases, but it is not a medical, legal, or emergency authority. |
| Is the subscription live? | PAYWALL DEPENDENT. The intended Apple-native offer is a 7-day trial, then monthly access, but publish only after StoreKit proof. |

## 7-Day Checklist

### Day 1 - Product Page Lock

- Choose final App Store name and subtitle.
- Choose one keyword field and one backup.
- Confirm support, privacy, and terms URLs.
- Freeze claims intentionally avoided.
- Metric: metadata ready for App Store Connect entry.

### Day 2 - Screenshot Capture

- Capture 6.9-inch Home, food/menu, Search, city/place, Saved, and Practice screens.
- Capture paywall only if StoreKit proof is complete.
- QA every screenshot for readable overlays, real app UI, no cropped controls, no hidden claims.
- Metric: 6 approved screenshots minimum.

### Day 3 - App Store Assembly

- Enter metadata.
- Upload screenshots.
- Add promotional text.
- Keep price out of long description.
- Metric: App Store Connect listing complete except any blocked paywall fields.

### Day 4 - Organic Launch Content

- Publish first food/menu short.
- Publish first search demo.
- Publish first static carousel with screenshots.
- CTA: `Download before Vietnam`.
- Metric: saves, shares, profile clicks, App Store product page views.

### Day 5 - Community And Beta/Soft Launch

- Share a practical, non-salesy post in travel-planning spaces if allowed by rules.
- Angle: `I built a Vietnam trip phrase/audio companion because translation apps do not tell you what to ask for.`
- Ask for testers/reviewers, not hype.
- Metric: qualified installs, tester comments, objections collected.

### Day 6 - Paid Test Prep

- Create two small ad concepts:
  - Food-first: menu confidence.
  - Before-you-land: useful phrase readiness.
- Use only captured native screenshots or screen recordings.
- Metric: creative-ready assets and landing URL.

### Day 7 - Review And Iterate

- Review App Store Analytics, campaign clicks, comments, and install quality.
- Choose next screenshot/product page test idea.
- Decide whether paywall copy can move from dependent to publishable based on StoreKit proof.
- Metric: top-performing hook, top objection, next test chosen.

### Short Script Outlines

#### Script 1 - Food/Menu

- Audience segment: food-curious first-time Vietnam traveler.
- Moment: they are planning meals and do not know menu names yet.
- Hook: `Before you order phở, hear how it sounds.`
- Channel: TikTok/Reels/Shorts.
- Offer/CTA: `Download SpeakLocal Vietnam before the trip.`
- Shots: Home > Food/Menu detail > audio tap > related phrase/save.
- Voiceover:
  - `I do not want a grammar lesson before Vietnam. I want to recognize the food, hear the name, and save the phrases I will actually use.`
  - `That is what SpeakLocal Vietnam is built for: food, places, and trip phrases in one native iPhone app.`
- Claim risk: do not imply every dish has equal audio depth; show only captured supported screen.
- Success metric: video saves and App Store clicks.
- Next action: record supported menu screen.

#### Script 2 - Search Trip Moment

- Audience segment: practical planners.
- Moment: they know a situation, not the phrase.
- Hook: `Search the trip moment, not a grammar book.`
- Channel: TikTok/Reels/Shorts.
- Offer/CTA: `Try it before you land.`

#### Script 4 - Practice

- Audience segment: beginner traveler who dislikes language courses.
- Moment: they want a small rehearsal before departure.
- Hook: `Practice the trip, not a textbook chapter.`

- Success metric: Practice opens and trial starts if paywall is live.
- Next action: capture Practice proof.

- Success metric: trial start rate, paid conversion, restore success.

# SpeakLocal Vietnam Audience And Positioning

Status: current marketing source of truth  
Last updated: 2026-05-18  
Scope: audience, positioning, offer framing, and copy guardrails for SpeakLocal Vietnam

## Core Audience

SpeakLocal Vietnam is for English-speaking travelers who are excited to go to Vietnam and want the country to feel more vivid, familiar, and usable before they arrive.

The primary user is planning or seriously considering a Vietnam trip. They are curious about food, coffee, menus, neighborhoods, landmarks, culture, and useful local phrases. They do not want a formal language course, and they are not trying to become fluent. They want a curated companion that helps them see what matters, hear how it sounds, save what they care about, and use it during the trip.

## Why They Want It

AI tools and Google Translate can produce translations on the fly, but they do not automatically give the traveler a well-curated Vietnam trip map. The traveler still has to know what to ask, which foods matter, which places belong, which phrases are worth saving, and how to make the experience feel coherent.

SpeakLocal earns its place by doing that curation ahead of time:

- curated Vietnamese travel phrases;
- food, coffee, menu, and drink discovery;
- city, landmark, place, and things-to-do context;
- playable Vietnamese audio;
- browse/search paths built around actual traveler moments;
- saved and practice loops for the user's trip;
- guided Messages-style conversations where they fit the app truth.

## Emotional Frame

The app should feel positive, practical, premium, and discovery-led. It can make Vietnam feel less intimidating, but it should never sell fear.

Use this emotional center:

- excited to go;
- curious to eat, explore, and understand;
- prepared before landing;
- confident enough to try;
- supported during the trip.

Avoid this emotional center:

- worry-led traveler;
- crisis translator;
- fear-heavy safety framing;
- fear of getting trapped or stuck;
- language-school pressure;
- fluency or mastery promises.

## Product Boundary

SpeakLocal is not:

- Google Translate;
- an arbitrary AI translation/chat product;
- Duolingo-style language learning;
- a full Vietnamese course;
- an emergency, legal, medical, or safety authority.

SpeakLocal is:

- a curated Vietnam travel companion;
- a practical phrase and audio guide;
- a sensory discovery layer for food, places, culture, and phrases;
- a native iOS app built around real trip moments.

## Offer Truth

Current monetization direction:

- 7-day free trial;
- then `$4.99/month`;
- subscription product ID: `app.speaklocal.vietnam.subscription.monthly`.

The trial should be earned by showing concrete value first: one useful phrase heard, one trip-relevant save, or a prepared Vietnam preview. Do not write customer-facing copy that implies a permanent free tier unless Jojo explicitly approves that product direction.

## Paid Value

People pay because SpeakLocal saves them from doing all the curation themselves. The subscription keeps the full Vietnam companion available for the trip: richer food/menu coverage, places and things to do, culture-aware phrase pages, playable audio, search/browse discovery, saved/practice loops, and useful trip support.

Do not sell the subscription as fear insurance. Sell it as keeping the richer, curated, audio-backed Vietnam experience in their pocket while they prepare and travel.

## Copy Rules

- Lead with Vietnam, food, places, culture, and useful phrases.
- Say "discover," "hear," "save," "practice," "before you land," and "for your trip."
- Use "less intimidating" only as a soft benefit, not as a fear hook.
- Avoid old safety-first phrasebook framing as the lead. Use "useful phrases," "trip phrases," "food phrases," or exact travel moments.
- Avoid "learn Vietnamese fast," "master Vietnamese," "become fluent," or course-style promises.
- Avoid "AI translator" positioning unless the product direction changes.
- Keep serious support surfaces calm and practical, not dramatic.

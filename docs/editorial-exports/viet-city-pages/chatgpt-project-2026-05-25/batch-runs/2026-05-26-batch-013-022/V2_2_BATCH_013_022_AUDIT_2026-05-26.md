## Audit Findings

Visible copy is generally better than the earlier Codex-written pass: most pages start from a concrete traveler moment, keep the reader view ahead of implementation notes, and use 2-3 reusable ready-audio phrase cards rather than one-off attraction phrases.

The main drift is not the visible prose itself; it is the handoff wrapper. Six captured outputs opened with a `Source basis`, `Source grounding`, or `Grounding note` line before the first listing. That makes the review surface feel more like a database or citation packet and encourages future sessions to repeat the habit.

No captured output should be treated as approved. Scores cluster around 27-29/30, with the common caps being sparse evidence, current venue/freshness checks, name-audio gaps, catalog QA, and unrun render/screenshot review.

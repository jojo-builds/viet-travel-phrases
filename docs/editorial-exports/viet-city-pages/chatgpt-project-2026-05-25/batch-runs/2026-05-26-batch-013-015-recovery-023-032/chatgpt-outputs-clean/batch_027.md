Reader View
Leave The City By The Coast Road
Bán đảo Sơn Trà is where Da Nang opens past hotels, bridges, and beach frontage. The road bends into forest, sea, weather, and wide coastal views that make the city feel bigger.

Useful phrase cards
“Hôm nay trời có mưa không?” — Will it rain today?
“Chỉ trên bản đồ giúp tôi được không?” — Can you show me on the map?
“Bạn có thể giúp tôi quay lại khách sạn được không?” — Can you help me get back to my hotel?
Sections
Choose The Road, Not Every Stop
The peninsula can turn into a list of viewpoints fast. Pick one coastal ride or one green stop, then leave room for weather, light, and the return.

Weather Changes The Visit

Clear light gives you the sea-and-city frame. Rain or fog makes the ride cooler and quieter, but it can flatten the view. Do not force a long loop if visibility disappears.

Pair Green With One City Edge

Sơn Trà sits naturally with Mỹ Khê Beach, Linh Ứng Pagoda, Lady Buddha, or a Hàn River evening. Choose one pairing instead of turning the day into a checklist.

Quận Sơn Trà / Son Tra District — Da Nang — Neighborhood
Reader View
A City Base With Peninsula Edges
Quận Sơn Trà is not one sight. It is the city side of the peninsula: coast road, hotel edges, cafes, small shops, lanes, and morning haze over green hills. The name helps beach-side Da Nang feel less anonymous.

Useful phrase cards
“Tôi chỉ xem thôi.” — I’m just looking.
“Đi bộ mất bao lâu?” — How long does it take on foot?
“Gọi taxi giúp tôi được không?” — Can you call a taxi for me?
Sections
Pick One Edge To Walk
Choose the Mỹ Khê side, a cafe cluster, or the road pointing toward Sơn Trà instead of trying to cover the whole district. The area makes more sense in short pieces.

Hotels, Cafes, Small Shops

This is the practical layer behind a beach-side stay: coffee, lanes, small shopfronts, scooter traffic, and the walk back to your room after dinner.

Know When It Becomes Sơn Trà

The district name and the peninsula name can blur. Quận Sơn Trà is the lived-in area; Bán đảo Sơn Trà is the greener road and sea frame beyond it.

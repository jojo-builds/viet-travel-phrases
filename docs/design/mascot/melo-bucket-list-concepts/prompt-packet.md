# Melo Bucket List Concepts Prompt Packet

Status: prompts used with Codex built-in image generation

## 1. Melo Base Sheet

```text
Create a polished app-mascot production concept sheet for Speak Local. Subject: Melo, a cute flat-brand chameleon mascot who travels country to country to learn languages. No text labels, no captions, no watermarks.

Style: modern flat vector app mascot, Dribbble-quality brand illustration, clean dark green outline, simple cel shadows, expressive friendly eyes, rounded non-scary body, corrected chameleon tail. Must not look plush, fuzzy, clay, realistic, or 3D rendered. Keep the same character identity across every pose.

Critical tail rule: every tail must be visibly attached at the rear hip, taper continuously, and curl forward/inward under the belly in an open C or hook shape. Keep a clear gap/open end and pointed/tapered tip. Do not make a full circle or tight spiral. Do not draw concentric rings. Do not draw anything that looks like a wheel, tire, bullseye, button, target, snail shell, or separate circular disc.

Layout: one clean warm off-white sheet with three rows.
Row 1: locked base views of Melo: front view, three-quarter view, side view, tiny app-icon silhouette.
Row 2: practice states: idle, listening with one small audio ripple, correct with warm smile, gentle correction with calm supportive expression, bucket list updated with small glow, hidden/rest with eyes closed. Keep phrase content absent.
Row 3: neutral base plus six shared unlock stages for a country bucket list: base neutral sage/cream, first phrases jade tint, city rhythm ceramic-blue patches, traveler confidence lotus-red accents, street-ready sign/stamp patterning, country attuned richer pattern coverage, fully unlocked vivid but tasteful country camouflage with jade, ceramic blue, lotus red, warm gold and soft glow.

Brand context: Speak Local is the brand; Speak Local Vietnam is the current app. Melo is shared across future country packs such as Japan, the Philippines, and Italy. Vietnam is only the first country color layer.

Avoid: fixed-path labels, flags, yellow star, hats, costumes, badges, coins, trophies, XP, confetti, leaderboards, realistic lizard texture, wet scales, scary reptile details, text.
```

## 7. Animation Storyboard

```text
Create a polished app-design animation storyboard for Melo, the Speak Local flat-brand chameleon. Show how the mascot animates in Practice while staying small and useful.

Most important mascot anatomy rule: the tail is attached to Melo's rear, tapers visibly, and curls inward toward the belly like a chameleon tail. It must never look like a wheel, tire, target, bullseye, separate disc, button, or concentric ring.

Layout: six horizontal animation strips, each with 3-4 small frames and motion arrows.
1. Idle: soft breathing, tiny eye blink, tail relaxes inward under belly.
2. Listening: head tilts, one subtle audio ripple, eye focuses, tail remains tucked inward.
3. Answer selected/checking: tiny lean forward, small pause, no excitement yet.
4. Correct: small smile, tiny color patch blooms, subtle Bucket List Stamp shimmer, no confetti.
5. Gentle correction: Melo lowers posture supportively, points with one hand, tail stays calm, no sad face.
6. Bucket List updated: brief warm glow and slightly brighter country pattern, then returns to compact idle pose.

Add a small side note visually: use Lottie/Rive-style vector layers, 12-24 frame loops, stable anchor point under belly, no layout shift.

Avoid: big victory pose, jumping, dancing, confetti, trophy, coins, XP, streaks, mascot in bottom chrome, scary lizard details, wheel tail, text-heavy diagram, watermark.
```

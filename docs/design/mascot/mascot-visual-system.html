SpeakLocal Chameleon Mascot Visual System

A calm travel companion that gradually picks up Vietnam motifs through real phrase practice. The mascot supports readiness; it never becomes the product, the score, or a costume.

Design stance

Keep the base silhouette constant, use jade and sea-glass as the main adaptation cue, reserve red and gold for small travel details, and remove or mute the mascot for sensitive phrase contexts.

Mascot Progression

Five states for art direction and native asset planning: neutral traveler, early Vietnam tint, mid-progress motif, completion motif, and the serious-context absence rule.

Base

Neutral chameleon traveler

Sage body, cream underside, small satchel. Reads as helpful and adult, not as a game mascot.

Early Vietnam

Jade route tint

Sea-glass body tint after a short source-anchored route. No badge, XP, confetti, or victory pose.

Mid-progress

Ceramic and lotus hints

Small scale pattern and travel-patch color. The silhouette stays familiar while details deepen.

Completion

Lantern-warm route mark

Completion gets warm light and refined motif density, not a costume, fireworks, or loud mascot takeover.

Sensitive

No mascot or neutral mark

Medical, police, harassment, safety, and high-stress prompts should privilege phrase clarity over warmth.

Native Flow Composites

These are not SwiftUI implementation files. They are compact product-direction composites that show scale, placement, restraint, and how the mascot fits the current glass-chrome app rhythm.

Native Translation Rules

The mascot should map into SwiftUI as a small asset family with state-driven variants, not as a new navigation model.

Keep

Stable silhouette, quiet route marks, local readiness language, phrase-page source links, and a native glass surface rhythm.

Avoid

No flag body paint, costume hats, coins, XP, streak pressure, leaderboards, childish triumph poses, or mascot in bottom/search chrome.

Subdue

Emergency, medical, safety, police, harassment, and high-stress prompts should hide the mascot or reduce it to a neutral progress mark.

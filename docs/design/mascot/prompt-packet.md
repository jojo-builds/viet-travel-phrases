## Prompt 1: Mascot Progression Sheet

Create a polished product-design mascot sheet for a premium native iOS travel phrase app called Speak Local Vietnam. The mascot is a calm chameleon traveler, refined and adult, not childish. Show five states on a clean warm white background:

1. Base neutral traveler with sage-green body, cream underside, rounded chameleon eye, curled tail, and a tiny canvas travel satchel.
2. Early Vietnam adaptation with restrained jade or sea-glass body tint and one quiet Bucket List Stamp.
3. Mid-progress Vietnam motif with subtle blue ceramic scale hints, a small lotus-red and warm-gold travel detail, and no costume.
4. High-progress/completion motif with lantern-warm light, tasteful jade depth, and refined motif density.
5. Sensitive-context subdued state showing the mascot absent or reduced to a neutral silhouette/progress mark.

Style: Apple-native product art, tactile, softly dimensional, clean edges, refined travel warmth, suitable beside Liquid Glass UI surfaces.

Avoid: flag body paint, stars as flag symbols, hats, costumes, coins, badges, XP, streak icons, confetti, fireworks, leaderboards, childish cartoon exaggeration, known character references, or any copyrighted IP resemblance. No text in the image.

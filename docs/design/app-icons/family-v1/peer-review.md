## Outcome

Status: PASS with follow-up simplification recommended before final production export.

The packet satisfies the task: it contains multiple rendered Vietnam icon directions, a family-system proof, Home Screen context, App Store context, small-size checks, and a clear recommendation.

## Findings

- Native iOS fit: Pass. The icons use rounded-square depth, high contrast, and polished glass/enamel materials that feel more premium than the old v1 placeholder icon family.
- Recognizability: Pass. Direction 5 is the most ownable because the same shape can read as chameleon, speech, route, and audio.
- Mascot risk: Pass with watchout. Direction 5 stays abstract enough to avoid a full mascot takeover. Direction 6 is memorable but should remain test-only.
- Small-size clarity: Pass with watchout. Direction 5 remains recognizable at 60 and 40 px, but the eye and route dots should be simplified before final production.
- Family scalability: Pass. The expanded family sheet shows a stable base mark with country-specific local layers for Vietnam, Philippines, Italy, Germany, and Japan.
- Premium tone: Pass. Direction 2 and Direction 5 feel most premium; Direction 3 is useful but too feature-icon-like for the main brand.
